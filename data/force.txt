Concept:
Force

Simple Explanation:
A force is a push or pull acting on an object.

Sindhudurg Example:
When fishermen pull a fishing net from the sea, they apply force.

Culture Connection:
Traditional fishing and farming activities use force every day.

Real Life Use:
- Machines
- Vehicles
- Sports
- Robotics

Think Question:
Can an object move without force?

Career Connection:
Robotics engineers use forces to control robots and drones.
