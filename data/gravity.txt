Concept:
Gravity

Simple Explanation:
Gravity is the force that pulls objects toward Earth.

Sindhudurg Example:
When a mango falls from a tree in Devgad, gravity pulls it toward the ground.

Culture Connection:
During mango harvesting season, farmers depend on understanding how fruits fall safely from trees.

Real Life Use:
- Falling objects
- Satellite orbits
- Aircraft and drones
- Sports

Think Question:
Why does a mango fall downward instead of upward?

Career Connection:
Aerospace engineers and drone developers must understand gravity when designing aircraft.

Challenge:
If gravity suddenly became half as strong, how would daily life change?
