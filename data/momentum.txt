Concept:
Momentum

Simple Explanation:
Momentum is the quantity of motion of an object.

Sindhudurg Example:
A fish transport truck moving on the highway has much more momentum than a bicycle moving at the same speed.

Culture Connection:
During local village processions, a large moving group is harder to stop suddenly than a single person.

Real Life Use:
- Vehicle safety
- Cricket
- Aircraft landing
- Drone navigation

Think Question:
Why is it harder to stop a truck than a bicycle?

Career Connection:
Engineers use momentum when designing vehicles, aircraft, and autonomous drones.

Challenge:
Which has more momentum: a slow truck or a fast bicycle?
